The building blocks

Keep the structure. Swap the rest.

LOFT is the part that doesn’t move. Every block around it has more than one option, so what you pick today, an AI vendor or a cloud, never turns into a rewrite later.

A request travels from your app, through your rules and LOFT, to an AI model. Four more blocks plug into LOFT from below. Every block except LOFT can be swapped.

    LIVE

    Bot definitions

    Change the bot without a release.

    What the bot says and how it behaves lives in a bot definition file in your own storage, not in the code. Your product team edits it; LOFT checks every new version and picks it up on a timer.

    support-bot.jsonIn your storage

    Instructions and model settings, for each bot and each model.

    bot definitionJSON
    "support-bot": {
  "gpt-4o-mini": {
    "systemMessage": "You are a friendly support agent. Never promise refunds.",
    "modelPreset": { "model": "gpt-4o-mini", "temperature": 0.3 }
  }
}

    Reusable texts, such as a greeting or a legal disclaimer, that any bot can use.

    Classify a ticket, extract an address, summarize a chat. Each runs once, with no chat memory.

    Safety & spend

    The cheapest model call is the one you never make.

      From one careful form to a whole agent team.
